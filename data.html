Purpose

This page documents the fields and allowed values in the Vermont Zoning Atlas. Where a field has an equivalent in the Vermont Zoning GIS Data Standard (Version 2.0, adopted November 12, 2025, revised March 11, 2026), the atlas uses the VCGI field name and domain values (full standard: files.vcgi.vermont.gov/other/standards-guidelines/zoning/zoning-standard.html), so the data can be combined with the statewide zoning dataset maintained by the RPCs and the State. The remaining fields follow the National Zoning Atlas methodology and capture detail the VCGI standard does not cover, such as setbacks, parking, and ADU rules.

25 of the 113 fields follow the VCGI standard. They are marked VCGI in the schema below.

Data Structure

Data has been broken into smaller files for ease in editing. In "data/RPC" all the zoning files are split out by Regional Planning Commission (RPC). These GeoJSONs can be explored in the abbreviated viewer on the homepage of this site or downloaded for further analysis. The RPC field in each file uses the VCGI code listed under Domains.

There is also a state-wide single file, "data/State of Vermont"; this is not the preferred way to update the files but rather for analysis.

If you are interested in viewing the legal PDFs downloaded as part of this process, explore "data/Town_Bylaw_Text" and the downloaded maps from town websites here: "data/Town_Zoning_Maps"

The machine-readable version of the schema on this page is analysis/canonical_fields.csv.

Collection Timeline and Data Quality

This effort is a collection of teams which includes volunteers, interns, and students. This was done over a year period from 2023-2024 and during this time updates to the zoning codes have already made some of this data inaccurate. It is likely that this data will only be updated once a year at most so this data can be out of date. It is also possible that the regulation from the State of Vermont may not be reflected in town zoning just due to the time it takes to update and comply with legislation and the very limited capacity many of these towns have to complete that work.

Specifications

Schema

The dataset contains 113 fields covering district identification, housing treatment, dimensional standards (setbacks, lot size, height, parking), and housing-specific regulations. Fields that follow the VCGI standard use VCGI's names; the remaining fields use a prefix system: F1F = single-family, F2F = 2-unit, F3F = 3-unit, F4F = 4+ unit, ADU = accessory dwelling unit, PRD = planned residential development, PUD = planned unit development. Renamed fields show their previous name.

The atlas records 4+ unit housing as one category, while VCGI separates 4-unit and 5+ unit housing. The 4+ value is written to both VCGI fields (for example UT4 and UT5) and marked VCGI · from 4+.
